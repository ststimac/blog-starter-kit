Herb salt is one of the easiest preservation methods and makes a lovely gift.

## What You Need

- Fresh herbs (rosemary, thyme, sage work beautifully)
- Coarse sea salt
- A clean jar

## How To Do It

1. Strip leaves from stems and chop finely.
2. Mix with salt in a 1:4 herb-to-salt ratio.
3. Spread on a baking sheet and dry in a low oven (200°F) for 30 minutes.
4. Store in an airtight jar.

Use it on roasted vegetables, grilled meat, or stirred into butter.
