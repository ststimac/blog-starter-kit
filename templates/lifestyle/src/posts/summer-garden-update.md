The tomatoes are finally ripening, the herbs are going wild, and I'm already planning what to plant for fall.

## What's Thriving

- **Tomatoes** — finally! The Pacific Northwest summer was slow to start but they're coming in now.
- **Basil** — I've been making pesto weekly.
- **Lavender** — ready to harvest and dry for winter.

## What's Struggling

The squash got powdery mildew. Lesson learned: more spacing next year and better air circulation.

## Fall Planning

I'm ordering garlic bulbs and thinking about cover crops for the beds that are done for the season.
