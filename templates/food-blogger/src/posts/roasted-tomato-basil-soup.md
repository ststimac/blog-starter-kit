  ingredients:
    - "2 lbs ripe tomatoes, halved"
    - "1 onion, quartered"
    - "4 cloves garlic"
    - "3 tbsp olive oil"
    - "2 cups vegetable broth"
    - "1/2 cup fresh basil"
    - "Salt and pepper to taste"
  instructions:
    - "Preheat oven to 400°F. Toss tomatoes, onion, and garlic with olive oil on a baking sheet."
    - "Roast for 35–40 minutes until caramelized."
    - "Transfer to a blender with broth and basil. Blend until smooth."
    - "Season with salt and pepper. Serve with crusty bread."

When tomatoes are at their peak, this soup is on repeat in my kitchen. Roasting concentrates their sweetness — you'll never go back to raw tomato soup.

## Tips

- Use a mix of tomato varieties for depth of flavor.
- Freeze in portions for winter — it tastes like summer in a bowl.
