  title: "Sourdough Focaccia"
  prepTime: "20 min"
  cookTime: "25 min"
  servings: "8"
  ingredients:
    - "500g bread flour"
    - "350ml warm water"
    - "100g active sourdough starter"
    - "10g salt"
    - "3 tbsp olive oil"
    - "Fresh rosemary and flaky salt for topping"
  instructions:
    - "Mix flour, water, starter, and salt. Rest 30 minutes."
    - "Stretch and fold every 30 minutes for 2 hours."
    - "Oil a pan, spread dough, dimple with fingers, top with rosemary."
    - "Proof 2 hours, bake at 450°F for 22–25 minutes."

There's nothing like pulling a golden focaccia from the oven. The dimples hold pools of olive oil — pure bliss.
