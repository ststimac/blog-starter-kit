It's the season of cozy and warm tones — my favorite time to get dressed.

## The Edit

I've been reaching for rich burgundies, camel, and cream. Layering is everything when the temperature swings between crisp mornings and warm afternoons.

## Styling Tip

Invest in one great coat. Everything else can be high-street — a good coat pulls any outfit together instantly.
